Instant Noodles

What You Need

  • 1 packet of instant noodles
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 egg
  • 1 green onion
  • 1 teaspoon of cooking oil

Steps

  1. Boil 2 cups of water in a pot.
  2. Add the instant noodles to the boiling water.
  3. Add the noodle seasoning and stir well.
  4. Be careful because the water is very hot.
  5. Add one egg and cook for 2 minutes.
  6. Stir the noodles gently and enjoy them while they are hot.
