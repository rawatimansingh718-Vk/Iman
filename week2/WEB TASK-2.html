Why I Love Cricket

By Iman Rawat | September 14, 2026

Cricket is my favourite sport because it is exciting, challenging, and teaches us the importance of teamwork. I enjoy watching and playing cricket whenever I have free time.

What I like most about cricket is that every match is different and unpredictable. It requires concentration, patience, and confidence. Playing cricket also helps me stay active and develop good teamwork skills.
